# Assumptions and open points

- The Annex-D breed list is stored from the supplied October-2024 legal attachment and corroborated by the October-2025 information sheet. The executable data captures eligibility tier and GEP flag, not the breeding-organisation name, because no rule supplied here depends on selecting that organisation.
- The 2026 drought notice changes the end of the holding period only. It explicitly preserves year-end animal-movement reporting; the current data model records the substantive holding outcome, while event reporting remains a structured discover-mode profile addition.
- The general sanction scale identifies possible percentages but does not allocate a precise percentage to each o6_5 breach. No spurious deterministic sanction rule is implemented.
- `eligible` is intentionally a profile-level decision only for the explicitly modeled automatic checks. Category-specific reproduction and transfer facts remain catalogued and exposed as discovery fields rather than inferred from aggregate livestock data.

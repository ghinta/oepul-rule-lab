# Assumptions and open implementation boundaries

- The sources do not prescribe a machine-readable allocation algorithm for the first-20-RGVE band where categories share a herder. `milk_premium` applies the illustrated ordering: up to 20 eligible milk RGVE receive the high milk tier within the capped total.
- The pro-rata rule for animals moved between alpine pastures does not prescribe a canonical data shape for multiple movement intervals. The proposed profile records final credited days.
- The 22 May drought notice provides an individual force-majeure route, not an automatic Behirtung exemption. Policy therefore never automatically waives daily water or care.
- HTML notices lack PDF page numbering, so their citations use a null page.

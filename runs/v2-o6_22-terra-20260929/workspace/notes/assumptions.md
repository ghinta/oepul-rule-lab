# o6_22 assumptions and open points

- The supplied measure sheet is the later operational source (October 2025); it is used for details that refine the 2024 special directive, notably the no-turn mixed/layered compost alternative and historical plan obligation.
- `continuous_use_days <= 366` implements “höchstens ein Jahr”. The source does not specify how leap years are administratively counted.
- The policy exposes deterministic checks for numerical and boolean evidence. Group-housing periods under the 1st Animal Husbandry Regulation, veterinary necessity, water permits and the adequacy of alternative turning equipment remain evidence-based checks and are retained as inputs rather than inferred.
- The 2026 drought notices concern arable/biodiversity, greening, nature and endangered-breed commitments; no notice changes an o6_22 requirement. The insecticide notice is likewise unrelated to pig welfare.
